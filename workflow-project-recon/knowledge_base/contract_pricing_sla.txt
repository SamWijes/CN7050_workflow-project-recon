SLA-RECON-01: Reconciliation strictly requires Quantity Dispatched = Quantity Installed + Quantity Quarantined + Quantity Returned for every item, and the dispatched quantity must match the carrier proof of delivery, before any invoice is routed for client sign off.

SLA-RECON-02: Any unused bulk material must be returned to the warehouse by the subcontractor and booked as a RETURN before the site can be reconciled.

SLA-RECON-03: Any serialized item that is dispatched but neither installed, quarantined nor returned must be investigated with the collecting engineer, quoting part number, serial number and outbound date.

SLA-BILLING-02: Any invoice line item that deviates from the contracted unit rate by more than the allowed variance must be flagged as a billing anomaly and held for Key Accounts Manager review.

SLA-BILLING-04: The contract does not fix a reconciliation or billing frequency. Reconciliation and billing frequency (for example at the end of the project, every N completed sites, or on request) must be agreed per project with the Key Accounts Manager before the first reconciliation. Each reconciliation batch is billed as one consolidated invoice with a section per site.

SLA-SIGNOFF-01: No invoice may be posted and no work order closed until both the Key Accounts Manager and the client have approved the billing package.
